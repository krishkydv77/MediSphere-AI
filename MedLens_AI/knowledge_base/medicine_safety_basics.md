# General medicine safety education
This file is basic educational content, not a prescribing reference.
- Use medicines only according to a clinician's instructions or the package directions appropriate for the specific product and patient.
- Check the exact medicine name, active ingredient, formulation, strength, expiry date and route before use.
- Do not use OCR guesses to make dose decisions. Confirm unreadable prescriptions with the prescriber or pharmacist.
- Ask a clinician/pharmacist about allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding, age, kidney/liver disease, other medicines, and relevant conditions.
- Do not start, stop, increase, decrease, split, crush, or combine medicines based solely on an AI response.
- Seek emergency care for severe allergic symptoms, trouble breathing, chest pain, fainting, severe bleeding, suspected overdose, or other severe/rapidly worsening symptoms.
